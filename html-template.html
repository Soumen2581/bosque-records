✦ ABOUT BOSQUE RECORDS ✦

Born from the shadows of ancient forests and the pulsating energy of psychedelic trance, Bosque Records is a sanctuary for deep, mystical soundscapes.

Founded in 2018 by a collective of forest-dwelling producers and visual artists, we curate experiences that bridge the earthly and the ethereal.

Our releases are more than music—they're sonic rituals designed to transport listeners to primordial groves where time bends and consciousness expands.
